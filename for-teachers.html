The straight answers

What you'd want to know
before showing it to 25 teenagers

01

Does it follow the actual study design?

Yes. Every concept maps to VCAA and NESA study designs — unit by unit, area of study by area of study. Where something is examinable in one state and not another, it says so on the page.

02

Do my students need accounts?

Not yet — the teacher experience (share links, class groups, present mode) is Release 2, and founding teachers get first say in it. If students want their own personalised study system at home, that's a separate student plan — entirely their call, never bundled into yours.

03

Can I use it for lesson planning?

Right now it's the best explain-it-again layer you'll find. Lesson building, class groups and progress views are next — and teachers on the platform get first say in how they work.
