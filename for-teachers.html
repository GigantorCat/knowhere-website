The straight answers

What you'd want to know
before showing it to 25 teenagers

01

Does it follow the actual study design?

Yes. Every concept maps to VCAA and NESA study designs — unit by unit, area of study by area of study. Where something is examinable in one state and not another, it says so on the page.

02

Do my students need accounts?

No. Put a concept on the projector, or send the class a share link: it opens in any browser, no login. If students want their own personalised study system at home, that's a separate student plan — entirely their call, never bundled into yours.

03

Can I use it for lesson planning?

Yes. Every Physics and Chemistry concept is a ready-made deck: the picture, a call it. round for the room, the takeaway and a homeplay task. Change the words, add your own slides, then present it or share it. More subjects from Term 1. Class groups and progress views are on the list — founding teachers get first say in how they work.

04

Will it work on the school network?

It's a normal website — nothing to install, on the projector laptop or theirs. Some school filters haven't categorised knowhere yet, so here's the one-page note for your IT team: the handful of domains to allow, and why.
