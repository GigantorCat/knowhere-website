knowhere vs Atomi vs Edrolo vs a tutor.

The short version

They're not the same product with different logos.

Atomi and Edrolo are content: a very good teacher explaining the syllabus on video, the same way to every student, plus practice questions. A tutor is a person: one hour a week of a human who can adapt to your kid. knowhere is a method: every concept in every subject rebuilt for the way your kid thinks — what motivates them, how their brain processes, and which mode makes it land. Different jobs. Sometimes you want two of them.

knowhere

What it is: every HSC and VCE concept as a living, interactive model, reframed for your kid's motivation, neurology and learning mode. Stuck? One tap, different explanation.

Costs: A$23–39/month, every subject on every plan. 7 days free, A$0 today.

Honest cons: launched September 2026. No track record, no video lessons, not inside your school. Built by one person. Judge it on the free week, not on us.

Atomi

What it is: thousands of bite-sized video and interactive lessons, smart quizzes, exam practice and AI feedback on answers. Widely used in schools.

Costs: A$59.99/month (up to 13 subjects) or A$470/year (up to 7). Free tier with sample lessons, no card, no time limit.

Honest cons: the lesson is the same lesson for everyone — the AI recommends which video, it doesn't change the video. If your kid doesn't learn by watching, that's the whole product.

Edrolo

What it is: a teacher-presented video for every syllabus dot point, plus hundreds of exam-style questions with sample answers and video solutions. Sold mostly through schools — 90% of top-performing schools, by their count.

Costs: A$150 per subject for 12 months, or A$60/month for up to 7 subjects. No free trial listed.

Honest cons: same video for every student. And check first — if the school already runs it, you may be paying twice.

A private tutor

What it is: a human. One subject, one hour a week, someone who notices when your kid's eyes glaze over and tries another way.

Costs: A$70–90/hour at a company like Cluey; A$35–70 for a uni student, A$90–110 for a qualified teacher, A$140+ for a specialist. Call it A$3,000 a year for one subject.

Honest cons: doesn't scale to six subjects, quality varies wildly, and the explaining stops when the hour does. Still the right call for some kids — see below.

Straight talk

When we're not the answer.

Your kid learns best from a person.

Get the tutor. Some kids need a human who'll notice the glazed look and won't let them off the hook. knowhere can sit underneath for the other five subjects, but it's not a replacement for that relationship.

The school runs Edrolo and your kid likes it.

Keep it — you're probably already paying for it in fees. The gap Edrolo leaves is the "explain it differently" one, which is exactly where knowhere lives. The two don't fight.

Your kid wants a teacher on video.

Atomi's videos are good and there are thousands of them. knowhere doesn't do lecture video — it does models you can poke, and audio, and discussion — so if watching is genuinely how your kid learns, start with Atomi's free tier.

They've tried everything and nothing sticks.

That's us. Nobody's scared of the subject — they've never been shown how they learn. The free week costs nothing and takes a minute to set up. If it doesn't click, it cost you a week.

Questions parents actually ask

The fine print, in plain English

Is knowhere a replacement for Atomi or Edrolo?

For some families, yes — every HSC and VCE subject is included on every plan, and it's cheaper. For others it's the layer underneath: Atomi or Edrolo for the lesson, knowhere for the moment the lesson didn't land.

Can I use knowhere alongside a tutor?

Yes, and it makes sense: a tutor for the one brutal subject, knowhere for all of them. The tutor gets a kid who arrives already knowing which bit didn't land.

Which is cheapest for a full Year 12 load?

knowhere: A$276–468 a year covers every subject. Atomi is A$470–720, Edrolo roughly A$720–750 for five subjects, and one tutor for one subject is around A$3,000. Prices as at 11 September 2026 — confirm before paying anyone.

Does knowhere have video lessons?

No. Every concept is an interactive model you can explore, available in four modes — see it, hear it, try it, or talk it through. If a video lecture is what your kid needs, Atomi or Edrolo do that well.

Is knowhere aligned to the actual HSC and VCE curriculum?

Yes — every concept maps to the NESA syllabus or VCAA study design your kid is examined on. Content that's examinable in one state and not the other is badged, not hidden.

What happens after the free week?

Nothing is charged today. Your first payment is 7 days later; cancel any time before then and you pay nothing. Monthly plans have no lock-in.
