## REPLIES YOU'LL NEED (so you don't have to think at 8pm)

- **"Is this only for Australia?"** — Right now, yes: HSC and VCE. The engine doesn't care what's loaded into it, so other curricula are a content job, not a rebuild. Year 12 is where it starts, not where it stops.
- **"How is this different from ChatGPT?"** — ChatGPT answers the question you asked. knowhere already knows how you learn and rebuilds the whole concept for that, before you've asked anything. Also it's mapped to the exact syllabus you're examined on, which ChatGPT will confidently get wrong.
- **"Learning styles are a myth."** — Agreed, VARK is dead. This isn't visual/auditory buckets. It's motivation (why you start), neurology (how you process) and mode (how it lands), and all four modes exist on every concept - the student isn't locked into one.
- **"Pricing?"** — A$23–39 a month, every subject on every plan. Less than an hour with a tutor. knowhere.me/compare has the honest version against the alternatives, cons included.
- **"Can I try it without a card?"** — The concepts on knowhere.me/experience-it, yes. The full app takes a card but charges $0 for 7 days.
