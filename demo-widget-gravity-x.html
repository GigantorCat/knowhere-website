Know This

A planet "orbiting" isn't being yanked back by an invisible rope. It's travelling in a straight line through curved space — and curved space looks like a circle from the outside.

THE KNOWDROP

Newton said: two masses pull on each other instantly, across any distance, with a force you can calculate exactly. It works brilliantly for rockets and tides — but it can't explain why light bends near the sun, or why Mercury's orbit drifts in a way Newton's maths can't predict.
