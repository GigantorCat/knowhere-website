The definition g = F/m gives units of N/kg (newtons per kilogram), which tells you the force per unit mass at any point in space. But N/kg is dimensionally equivalent to m/s²—both describe acceleration. This dual identity reveals a deep truth: gravitational field strength is numerically equal to gravitational acceleration. On Earth's surface, saying "the field strength is 9.81 N/kg" is identical to saying "objects accelerate at 9.81 m/s²." One concept, two perspectives.
Know This
Gravitational field strength g = GM/r² decreases with the square of distance from the center, measured in N/kg or equivalently m/s², and always points toward the mass creating the field.
