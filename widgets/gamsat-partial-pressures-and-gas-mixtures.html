Partial pressures and gas mixtures: a gas chamber and a partial-pressure chart that change with total pressure and gas mix

Everest air is still 21% oxygen. It just pushes less.

In a mixture of gases, each gas pushes on the walls as if the others were not there, so its partial pressure is its mole fraction times the total pressure. Lower the total pressure to the top of Everest, then raise it to 30 m under the sea, and watch which number moves and which one holds still.

Know this

In a mixture, each gas has a partial pressure equal to its mole fraction times the total, and it diffuses down its own partial-pressure gradient, not a percentage gradient.
