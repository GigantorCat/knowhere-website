Germans did resist the regime: communists, pastors, teenagers, students and finally generals. None of it grew into a mass movement, and the reason sits in the geometry of a police state. Suppose a lone cell links itself to two others: is it safer, or more exposed? Raise the coordination between the cells and watch what happens to the linked ones when the sweeps arrive.
