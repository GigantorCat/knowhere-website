Density done well means more people per hectare, shorter distances between home and services, and reduced per-capita infrastructure. When paired with mixed-use zoning, compact development cuts car dependence, shrinks energy bills and enables district heating networks that recover waste heat. The arithmetic is direct: double the density and you typically halve the land consumed for housing, roads and parking while energy use per person drops by 30–40%. High-density precincts also concentrate demand enough to support public transport, walking and cycling infrastructure that sprawl can never sustain.
Know This
A city's ecological footprint is the land area needed to supply its resources and absorb its waste — and sustainable design shrinks it through compact density, transit networks, and circular resource flows.
