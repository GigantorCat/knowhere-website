Paying the average price makes the best cars vanish

A buyer who cannot check a used car protects themselves by offering the average value of everything on sale. The owner of a genuinely good car refuses that offer and keeps it, so the average falls, the offer falls and the next-best cars walk too. Widen the information gap and watch which cars leave first and where the offer line settles.

Know This
When sellers do not disclose what they know, buyers pay the average price, the best cars leave and the average falls again: that unravelling is adverse selection, a market failure that leaves quality goods underprovided.
