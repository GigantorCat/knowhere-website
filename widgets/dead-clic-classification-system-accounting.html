Interactive T-account showing which side increases each account element under the DEAD CLIC rule

Drawings belongs to the owner, yet it grows with a debit

DEAD CLIC sorts every ledger account by the side that makes it bigger, and the first letter of each word is that side: Debit increases Expenses, Assets and Drawings; Credit increases Liabilities, Income and Capital. Bigger means a bigger account, not a better result for the owner. Choose an account and whether it increases or decreases, then raise the amount and watch which column of the T-account fills.
