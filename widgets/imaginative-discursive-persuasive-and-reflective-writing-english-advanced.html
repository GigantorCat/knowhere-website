Blend the forms, keep the promise

Imaginative, discursive, persuasive and reflective writing are four types of writing, and each one promises its reader something different. Strong writers blend them on purpose, lending a scene to an argument or an argument to a reflection. Switch the type of writing, then add a blend, and watch which sentences keep the promise.
