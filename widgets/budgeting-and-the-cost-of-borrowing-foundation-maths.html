The smallest monthly repayment can be the most expensive loan

A budget sorts your pay into fixed costs you cannot dodge, discretionary spending you can, and whatever is left. Borrowing then adds a second price to anything you buy: interest and fees on top of the amount you took. Raise the variable spending until the household tips into deficit, then switch to the loan, stretch the term and watch what happens to the total repayable.
