CSR in operations means going beyond legal sourcing, waste disposal, and safety standards. Businesses audit supply chains for labour conditions, reduce environmental footprint beyond regulatory minimums, and invest in ethical suppliers even when cheaper alternatives exist. The compliance floor covers minimum environmental permits and occupational health regulations; the ethical ceiling asks whether every link in the supply chain reflects the company's stated values.

Know This

Legal compliance is the baseline floor; corporate social responsibility asks what the business owes beyond it—through ethical operations, truthful marketing, and fair people management.
