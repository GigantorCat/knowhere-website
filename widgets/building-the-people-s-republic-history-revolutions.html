Land reform was about more than redistribution — it was revolutionary violence as policy. Between 1950 and 1952, the CCP organised "speak bitterness" meetings where peasants publicly accused landlords of exploitation. These gatherings often ended in immediate execution. The party deliberately fostered violence to destroy the old class structure and bind peasants to the revolution through shared complicity. An estimated 1-2 million landlords died. The violence wasn't incidental — it was the mechanism of transformation, ensuring no return to the feudal past.
Know This
The CCP used organised violence as a tool of state-building — land reform didn't just redistribute property, it physically eliminated the landlord class to ensure irreversible change.
