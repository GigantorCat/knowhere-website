A wider window is not a smoother line unless it matches the season

A time series is three things stacked on top of each other: a long-term trend, a seasonal swing that repeats on the calendar, and short irregular wobble. A moving mean is meant to peel away the swing and the wobble, but it only cancels the season when its window matches the season length. Widen the window from 6 to 9 to 12 and watch the smoothed line; then add an outlier or a structural change and watch what survives the smoothing.

Know This
A moving mean cancels seasonality only when its window equals the seasonal period; it smears an outlier and blurs a structural change rather than removing them.
