When health is optimal, the individual gains direct access to quality of life. You can work consistently, maintain relationships with energy and presence, and participate in activities that matter. Physical and mental capacity aren't drained by illness or limitation. The exam wants you identifying these personal outcomes — employment stability, social connection, autonomy in daily tasks. Health isn't an abstract metric; it's the resource that lets you show up for your own life and contribute to the lives around you.

Know This

Optimal health enables participation and productivity at both scales: individuals gain work capacity, relationship quality, and daily autonomy, while nations reduce healthcare costs and increase economic output — health is a resource for everyday life, not just an absence of disease.
