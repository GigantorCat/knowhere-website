The form you choose is a contract

Every mode makes a silent promise to your reader. Imaginative writing builds worlds, discursive writing thinks aloud, persuasive writing stakes a position, reflective writing examines process. Switch modes mid-essay and you break the contract. Select a mode below to see what each form promises.

Voice, image, and structure are the three pillars of imaginative craft. Voice establishes the narrator's distance and reliability. Image builds sensory texture—not decoration, but the concrete detail that makes abstraction feel real. Structure determines when information arrives: chronology, fragmentation, layering. All three work together to hold your reader inside the world you're building, maintaining the illusion that this is happening now, to someone real.

Know This

Strong writers recognise that switching writing modes mid-piece breaks the reader contract—choose your mode at the start and sustain its core promise all the way through.
