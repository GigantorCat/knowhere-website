Fertility and mortality: five population indicators compared across youthful, transitional and aged populations

The longest-lived population has the higher death rate

Crude death rate counts deaths per thousand people a year, and a population full of old people has many people near the end of life, however long those lives have been. Infant mortality and life expectancy tell the health story the crude rate hides, and births minus deaths tells the growth story the birth rate hides.
