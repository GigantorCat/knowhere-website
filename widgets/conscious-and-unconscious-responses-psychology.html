Highlight a pathway

Both responses begin identically: a receptor in the skin fires and a sensory neuron, part of the somatic nervous system in the peripheral nervous system, carries the message into the spinal cord. Inside the cord the message is handled twice. An interneuron passes it straight to a motor neuron, which contracts the arm muscle: the spinal reflex, complete at about 70 ms in this model. At the same time the message is relayed up the cord to the brain, which registers the heat and sends its own command back down the cord to a motor neuron, finishing at about 200 ms. The spinal cord therefore has two roles here: it initiates the reflex and it relays messages to and from the brain.

Know This

A spinal reflex is initiated in the spinal cord before the brain is involved, while a conscious response is processed by the brain and still travels through the spinal cord both ways.
