Before it is a number, a probability is a count

Nobody picks a probability. When every outcome has the same chance, you count how many outcomes you want and compare that with how many there are: that fraction is the probability. Raise the favourable count and watch the row light up and the marker move along the 0-to-1 line; then raise the total and watch the same count shrink into a smaller fraction.
