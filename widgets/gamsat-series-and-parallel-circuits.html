Add a resistor in parallel and the total resistance falls

In series, charge has one path, so the same current passes every point and the supply voltage is shared out. In parallel, each branch is its own path across the full voltage, so the branch currents add and the total resistance drops below the smallest branch. Pick a circuit, then raise or lower R₂ and watch the charge and every meter respond.
