Protection always costs someone. The only question is who gets the bill

An import-competing market has a demand line, a supply line and a flat world price, and imports fill the gap between them. Every protection tool works by moving one of those three things. Raise the tariff and watch the import gap close, then switch to a subsidy and watch what the price does.

Know This
Tariffs, quotas and subsidies all raise domestic output and shrink imports, but a tariff or quota makes consumers pay a higher price while a subsidy keeps the world price and sends the bill to taxpayers.
