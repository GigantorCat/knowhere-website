When all four mechanisms align, employees receive consistent signals about which behaviours truly matter. Hiring decisions filter for cultural fit, demonstrating entry standards. Reward systems reinforce desired behaviours through tangible recognition. Dismissal decisions define hard boundaries by showing which violations trigger consequences. Physical space design embeds cultural messages in daily environments, like open floor plans signalling transparency values or removing executive perks demonstrating equality.
Know This
Symbolic actions by leadership create powerful culture signals, like CEOs removing executive parking spaces to demonstrate equality values — but only when recruitment, dismissal decisions, and reward systems consistently reinforce the same message.
