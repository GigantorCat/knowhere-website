Heart valves have no muscle. Every one is opened by pressure.

Through one beat, the left ventricle, the aorta and the left atrium each trace their own pressure, and a valve flips only where two of those traces cross. Drawn as pressure against volume, the same beat becomes a loop with a corner at every valve event.
