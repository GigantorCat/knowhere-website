The slope of a product is not the product of the slopes

Multiply two functions and the gradient is two pieces added, not two slopes multiplied. Divide them and a minus sign appears; put one inside another and the slopes really do multiply. Know this

A product needs u′v + uv′, a quotient needs (vu′ − uv′)/v², and a function inside a function needs the chain rule, so read the outermost operation first and work inwards.
