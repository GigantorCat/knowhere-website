Electric fields accelerate - each pass through an RF cavity adds energy. The particle crosses a gap where the electric field oscillates at radio frequency; if it arrives at the right phase, the field kicks it forward and it leaves faster than it entered. Lap after lap, the energy compounds. The field does work on the particle, converting electrical potential energy into kinetic energy, and the particle gains speed - until relativity takes over and most of the added energy becomes mass-energy instead of velocity. Magnetic fields cannot do this: they deflect but never accelerate, because the Lorentz force is always perpendicular to motion.
Know This
Electric fields accelerate - each pass through an RF cavity adds energy; magnetic fields steer and focus but add none, while collision energy converts to mass through E = mc² run forward.
