Two rules, two lines, one shared point

Every non-vertical line is y = mx + c: m is the rate of change, c is the value at x = 0. Put a second line on the same axes and the pair can meet once, never, or everywhere. Raise the gradient, then the intercept, and watch where the green line cuts the axes and where it meets the amber one.

Know This
Two lines meet where both rules give the same y: set mx + c equal to the other rule, solve for x, substitute to find y, and that point of intersection is the simultaneous solution.
