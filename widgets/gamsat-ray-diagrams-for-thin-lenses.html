Move the object in towards F and a convex lens throws its image further away

A pinhole makes a picture with no glass at all: light travels in straight lines through one small hole and lands upside down on the screen behind it. A thin lens does the same job by bending light, and three rays from the top of the object are enough to find where its image forms. Start with the convex lens, bring the object in towards the focal point and watch where the image goes.
