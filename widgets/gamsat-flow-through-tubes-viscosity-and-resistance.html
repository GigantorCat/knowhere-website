Halve the radius and the flow falls to a sixteenth

Fluid sticks to the wall of a tube and rubs on itself, so it races down the middle and stands still at the edge. That friction, called viscosity, makes flow depend on the radius to the fourth power, not on the area. Narrow the vessel and watch the flow collapse, then switch the fluid to water and watch the same tube open up.
