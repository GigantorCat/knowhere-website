Three rungs up the ladder, still nobody has decided

A workplace dispute climbs a ladder: a conversation under the grievance procedure, then a mediator, then the Fair Work Commission conciliating, then the Commission arbitrating, then a court. Most of that ladder exists to help the two sides reach their own deal, not to rule on it. Raise the escalation step and watch where the decision ring sits and when the rung turns solid.
