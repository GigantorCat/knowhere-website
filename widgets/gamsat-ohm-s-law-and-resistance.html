Ohm's law is a straight line only while the temperature holds steady

Voltage pushes the free electrons in a wire; the vibrating metal ions get in their way, and that is resistance. Keep the wire at one temperature and double the push gives double the current. Raise the voltage, then change the wire and watch the line tilt.
