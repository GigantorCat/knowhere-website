Energy doesn't convert — it just swaps

In a perfect vacuum with no air resistance, energy conservation is exact: every joule of gravitational potential energy converts completely into kinetic energy. The total mechanical energy Eg + Ek remains absolutely constant throughout the fall. This is why physicists often start with frictionless models — not because they're realistic, but because they reveal the underlying conservation principle without complicating factors. At the top of the fall, all energy is stored as potential (mgh) and the object is stationary. At the bottom, all that energy has become motion (½mv²) and height is zero.

Know This

Energy is never created or destroyed — it only changes form, and during free fall, gravitational potential energy trades perfectly with kinetic energy so that their sum stays constant.
