Interactive visualisation of the pyruvate fork: oxygen sends pyruvate into the mitochondrion for about 30 ATP, while no oxygen sends it to fermentation in the cytosol, ending in lactic acid in animals or ethanol and carbon dioxide in yeast

Fermentation makes no ATP. It makes NAD⁺

Know This
In both animals and yeast, fermentation in the cytosol regenerates NAD⁺ so glycolysis keeps making its 2 ATP; only the product differs: lactic acid in animals, ethanol and CO₂ in yeast.
