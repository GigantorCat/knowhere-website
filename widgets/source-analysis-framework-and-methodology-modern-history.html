A propaganda poster is poor evidence until you change the question

Reliability is not a property of a source. It belongs to the pairing of a source with a question. A poster made to persuade is weak evidence of what happened in the street and strong evidence of what the state wanted believed. Change the question, then move across the five source types, and watch what each one can and cannot prove.

Know This
No source is reliable in itself: its value depends on the question you ask of it, and its limitations are whatever that question needs and the source cannot supply.
