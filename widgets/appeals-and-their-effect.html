Weak: "The writer uses an appeal to fear when they say 'our children will inherit an uninhabitable planet'." This just names the device without explaining why it works or who it targets. It's description, not analysis. Year 12 responses need to connect the appeal to the specific audience's values and show how the emotional trigger drives the argument's logic. Naming is the starting point, not the endpoint.
Know This
Appeals target emotion, not just logic — fear makes threats feel immediate, fairness invokes moral debt, authority borrows credibility, and self-interest converts ideology into personal gain.
