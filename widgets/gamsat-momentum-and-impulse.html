Interactive momentum and impulse visualisation showing how collision time affects peak force

Same stop, same momentum change. So what does the time do?

Airbags, crumple zones and bent knees all play with one thing: how long a collision lasts. The momentum change is set by how fast you were going. Change the collision time and see what the force graph and the peak force do.

Know This

Impulse equals the area under a force-time graph, even when the force varies, and it always equals the momentum change.
