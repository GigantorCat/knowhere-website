Dilute it ten times and not one ion leaves

Concentration is an amount of solute shared out through a volume of solution: c = n ÷ V. Add water and the count of solute particles stays exactly the same; only the volume they share grows. Make the solution up to a new volume and watch the ions spread out, then switch between making a standard solution and diluting a stock.

Know This

Adding water changes the volume but never the moles of solute, so the concentration falls in proportion and c₁V₁ = c₂V₂.
