The SCLC under Martin Luther King Jr. exemplified hierarchical organising: centralised moral authority radiating from a single charismatic leader. It excelled at a unified public message, large coordinated actions like the March on Washington, and a clear spokesperson for the press. But that structure concentrated risk — when King was assassinated in 1968 the SCLC struggled to keep momentum without his singular voice.

Know This

Network resilience depends on topology. Distributed networks survive targeted attacks because power is diffuse — there is no single node whose removal collapses the whole structure.
