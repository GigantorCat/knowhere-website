SDG 1 No Poverty increases household income, which directly purchases nutritious food and healthcare access. This improved nutrition reduces infant mortality rates while better healthcare access lowers disease burden. The economic security also reduces maternal stress during pregnancy, improving birth outcomes. These effects compound: children who survive infancy with better nutrition show enhanced cognitive development, creating long-term intellectual capacity improvements that weren't the direct target of the poverty intervention.

Know This

Each SDG functions as an intervention point in an interconnected system where improvements in one dimension create cascading effects across multiple aspects of human development throughout the lifespan.
