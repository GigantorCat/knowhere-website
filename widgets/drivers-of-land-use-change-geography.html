Drivers push on farmland. Terrain and planners push back

A parcel on the edge of a growing town does not change for one reason. Rising land value, new households and a new transport link each push cells from farmland to housing, while the hill behind the parcel and the planning strategy decide how far that push gets. Add years, switch the drivers on one at a time, and watch which cells convert first and which ones never do.
