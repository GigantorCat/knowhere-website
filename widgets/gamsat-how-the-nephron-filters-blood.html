Interactive nephron: blood flows into the glomerulus, small particles are pushed into Bowman's capsule and flow along the tubule. Useful particles are reabsorbed into the capillary, extra wastes are secreted in, and urine drains from the collecting duct. Set the blood pressure in the glomerulus and switch between following reabsorption and following secretion.

Your kidneys filter out the good stuff too, then grab it back

Blood pressure squeezes water and small molecules out of the glomerulus into Bowman's capsule, useful and useless alike. The tubule then takes back what the body needs and adds extra wastes, and whatever is left is urine. Raise the pressure and watch more get squeezed out, then switch lenses to follow each particle's fate.

Know This

The nephron removes waste in three steps: pressure drives glomerular filtration, the tubule takes back what the body needs by selective reabsorption, and extra wastes are added by secretion.
