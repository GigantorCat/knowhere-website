Financial KPIs like market share and net profit tell you the outcome — whether you're making money and winning customers. These are the numbers investors and boards care about because they directly measure commercial success. But they're lagging indicators: by the time net profit drops, the damage is already done. You can track them monthly or quarterly, but they show you where you've been, not where you're going. Smart businesses pair financial KPIs with operational ones to get the full picture.
Know This
KPIs are measurable indicators of performance — specific numbers like market share, net profit, productivity growth, staff turnover, absenteeism, wastage, and complaints that diagnose whether a business needs to change.
