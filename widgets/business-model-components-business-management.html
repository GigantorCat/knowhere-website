Value proposition defines what problem the business solves for customers and why they would choose this solution over alternatives. It's the promise at the core of the model—the reason customers exchange money for your offering. Examiners expect you to articulate this clearly: what specific pain point does the business address, and what makes the solution compelling enough to justify the price? A strong value proposition directly determines which revenue streams are viable and influences the cost structure needed to deliver it.
Know This
The model maps the relationship between customer needs, value proposition, revenue mechanisms, and cost structure into a coherent system where changes in one element cascade through the entire model.
