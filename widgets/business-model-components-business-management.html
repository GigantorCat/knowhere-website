Interactive business model chain: paying customers times price gives revenue, minus fixed and variable costs gives profit or loss

A business can take $20k a month and still be losing money

A business model is a chain. The value proposition decides who pays and how much, revenue is customers times price, and the cost structure splits the cost of serving them into a fixed part that never moves and a part that grows with every customer. Raise or lower the number of paying customers and watch revenue, costs and the profit bar move together; then switch to the premium price and watch where the break-even marker lands.
