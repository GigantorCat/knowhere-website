Effectiveness High

Local responses (community reforestation, urban greening, catchment restoration) are highly adaptive and directly accountable to affected populations, but they struggle with coordination across boundaries and often lack funding. A single council area protecting riparian vegetation can't control upstream deforestation. Effectiveness depends on whether the scale matches the process — local erosion control works; local climate mitigation doesn't. Always ask: does this scale match the system being changed?

Know This

Every management response generates its own set of environmental, social, and economic impacts — evaluation isn't optional, it's the geographical insight that separates description from analysis.
