The writer uses a rhetorical question, inclusive language, and expert opinion. They also use imperatives and emotive language about flooding and lives. These techniques make the passage persuasive. This response earns almost nothing because it only identifies devices without explaining their intended effect on the reader. Naming a technique proves you can spot patterns, but analysis requires arguing about how those patterns position the audience to think or feel. The difference between identification and analysis is the difference between describing a tool and explaining what it builds.
Know This

Identifying a device is not analysing it — always explain the intended effect on the reader and argue about positioning, not presence.
