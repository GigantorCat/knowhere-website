Between meals, your blood sugar comes from your liver

Two hormones from the pancreas tell the liver whether to store glucose or release it, and that tug of war holds blood glucose between about 4 and 7.8 mmol/L. Break one side of the loop and the level drifts out of range. Pick a body and an event, then move the clock forward and watch blood glucose, insulin and glucagon.
