Trotsky built the Red Army from nothing into a 5-million-strong force by 1920. He re-recruited 50,000 Tsarist officers under threat of death, instituted military discipline with execution for desertion, and deployed Bolshevik commissars to every unit to ensure loyalty. The Red Army's central position allowed it to defeat White forces on interior lines, moving troops rapidly between fronts while the Whites remained geographically isolated from each other. Trotsky's armoured trains became mobile command centres, carrying him to crisis points across the vast front.

Know This

The Civil War (1918-1921) saw the Reds defeat the Whites through Trotsky's Red Army, the Cheka's terror, and War Communism – but victory left the economy in ruins and the peasantry alienated.
