Most assistive devices repair nothing: they route around one broken step.

A cochlear implant, a dialysis machine and a pacemaker leave the damaged tissue exactly as it is. Each finds the one stage of a pathway that has stopped working and either bypasses it into healthy tissue downstream or does its job outside the body.
