On a log scale, every equal step means ten times

Put 0.001 and 1,000,000 on an ordinary ruler and nearly everything gets crushed against zero. A base 10 log scale spaces the powers of ten evenly, so each step along it multiplies by 10. Change the value and watch it move on both rulers at once.

Know This

On a base 10 log scale each equal step multiplies by 10, so moving up one order of magnitude means a value ten times bigger.
