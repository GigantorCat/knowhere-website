Integration by parts: choose which factor is u, step through each pass of the formula and derive it from the product rule

Parts is the product rule, read backwards

Differentiate a product and you get two terms. Integrate that line and rearrange it, and you have ∫u dv = uv − ∫v du: a product swapped for a boundary term and a new integral. The whole skill is choosing u so the new integral is simpler than the one you started with.
