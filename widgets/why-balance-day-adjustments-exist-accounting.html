Balance day adjustments: how one rent payment is split between an expense and an asset or liability at balance day

You paid $6000 on day 1. By day 30 only half of it is an expense.

Accrual accounting records an expense when the benefit is used up, not when the cash moves. Rent of $6000 paid on day 1 buys 60 days of the premises, so at balance day that $6000 has to be split between the days already used and the days still to come. Move the balance day along the rent period, switch the adjustment off, and watch what happens to the expense row and the profit line.
