Know This

A free body diagram shows only the forces acting ON the object — weight (mg downward), normal reaction (perpendicular to the surface), friction (opposing motion) and tension (along ropes) — and for equilibrium you resolve forces into perpendicular components and set the sum to zero in each direction.
