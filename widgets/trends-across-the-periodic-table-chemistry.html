Add protons and atoms get smaller

Step across a period and every element has one more proton and one more electron than the last, yet the atom shrinks. Step down a group and it swells again. Those two gradients run under every trend across the periodic table. Pick a property to recolour the table, then step through the elements and watch the radius shrink across each period.

Know This
