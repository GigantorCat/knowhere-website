Amplifying drivers — technological obsolescence, competitive pressure, regulatory deadlines — creates urgency but often triggers defensive reactions. When you push harder for change without addressing barriers, restraining forces strengthen in response. Employees dig in when they lack skills for new systems, finance teams block unfunded initiatives, cultural norms reassert themselves. The equilibrium barely shifts because every increase in driving force meets equal and opposite resistance. Change fatigue sets in, momentum stalls, and the organisation returns to baseline despite heroic effort.

Know This
Strategic change management succeeds by weakening specific restraints through training, communication, or phased implementation rather than only intensifying the drivers pushing for change.
