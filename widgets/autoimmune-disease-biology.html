To stop an immune attack, send in another antibody

Autoimmune disease is not the whole immune system turning on the whole body. A few lymphocytes lose tolerance to one self-antigen and destroy only the cells that carry it, while every other tissue is left alone. Lower the self-tolerance and watch which cells the T cells go for, then switch the monoclonal antibody on and watch what happens to the attack.
