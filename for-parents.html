Not magic. Models.

Personalisation with actual integrity

"Personalised learning" usually means a font-size setting and a first name in an email. knowhere is built differently: three models read how your child learns, and every explanation, example and quiz is constructed from them. The curriculum content never changes — only the way in does. That's the honest version of personalisation, and it's the only one that works.

VCE and HSC are two-year programs. knowhere starts where the stakes peak — Units 3&4 — with Year 11 (Units 1&2) landing in Release 2, included in every plan. And nothing examinable is ever diluted: the roughly 300 concepts that carry another state’s content are clearly badged “not examinable in your state”.

Motivation — why they start

Nine motivational types. What hooks a competitive kid into calculus isn't what hooks a deep-thinker. knowhere finds theirs and leads with it, every single concept.

Neurology — how they process

ADHD, ASD, dyslexia — treated as processing differences with real strengths, not deficits. The format is rebuilt around their brain: shorter sprints, explicit rules, audio-first.

Mode — how it lands

See it, hear it, try it, or talk it through. Every concept is available in all four modes, leaning into the one where it actually clicks for them.

Built different, on purpose

Designed by a neurodivergent founder

Most learning platforms are built for a hypothetical "average student" — then accessibility gets bolted on at the end, if at all. knowhere started from the opposite assumption.

knowhere was designed by a neurodivergent founder, for neurodivergent students and typical neurology alike. If your child has ADHD, is autistic, or has dyslexia, the platform doesn't ask them to learn like someone else — it reshapes the format so their strengths do the lifting. And if their neurology is typical, they simply get learning tuned to how they think. Either way: an equal playing field. Not a special version. The same excellent one, shaped differently.
